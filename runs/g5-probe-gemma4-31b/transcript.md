This is a high-stakes procurement dispute. To protect the District from litigation and ensure the integrity of the bidding process, the following synthesis summarizes the key legal findings and recommends a path forward.

### Executive Summary
The District is facing a formal protest from Tasker & Moye Construction regarding the apparent low bidder, Corvane Builders. The core of the dispute centers on **Responsiveness** (whether the bid follows the rules) and **Responsibility** (whether the bidder is capable of performing the work).

### Key Legal Findings

**1. Responsiveness: The Subcontractor Gap**
*   **The Issue:** Corvane failed to list subcontractors for the Fire Alarm/Sprinkler System (~$740k) and HVAC/Roofing (~$1.9M).
*   **The Law:** Public Contract Code § 4104 generally requires bidders to identify subcontractors for scopes exceeding $25,000. 
*   **The Risk:** A Class B license (Corvane) does not authorize self-performance of specialized Fire Sprinkler work ($C-16$ requirement). If Corvane intended to self-perform without a licensed sub, the bid is likely non-compliant with licensing laws and specification requirements.

**2. Responsiveness: The Addendum Oversight**
*   **The Issue:** Corvane acknowledged only Addendum No. 1, while Tasker & Moye acknowledged both No. 1 and No. 2.
*   **The Law:** Bids must typically acknowledge all addenda to ensure the bidder has priced the modified scope correctly.
*   **The Risk:** Failure to acknowledge a mandatory addendum can render a bid non-responsive. If Addendum 2 changed the scope, Corvane may have under-priced the project, leading to an "unbalanced bid."

**3. Responsibility: Licensing and Ability**
*   **The Law:** Under PCC § 10314, the District has discretion to reject bids from contractors deemed "irresponsible."
*   **The Risk:** If Corvane cannot produce licensed subcontractors for these major trades by the award date, they may be deemed irresponsible.

### Public Records Compliance
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act and Government Code § 6253, bid documents are public records upon opening. The District must fulfill the request for Corvane's documents by noon on the date requested to maintain transparency and mitigate litigation risk regarding record access.

**2. Due Diligence: The Written Inquiry**

*   Confirmation of how they intend to satisfy the licensing requirements for Fire Sprinklers and HVAC/Roofing.

**3. Final Determination**
*   **If Curable:** If Corvane can provide licensed subcontractors and a valid explanation for the addendum before the contract is executed, the District may proceed.
